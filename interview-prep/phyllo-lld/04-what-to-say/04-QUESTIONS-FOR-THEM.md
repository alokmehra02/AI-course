# Questions to Ask Them (End of Round)

Ask 1–2 only. Prefer specific over generic.

## Strong options for Phyllo

1. “For Connect sync, how do you think about freshness SLAs differently across Identity vs Content vs Income?”  
2. “On webhook delivery, do you optimize more for throughput or per-tenant fairness when one customer endpoint is slow?”  
3. “When adding a new work platform, what’s the hardest part in practice — OAuth quirks, rate limits, or schema normalization?”  
4. “How split is the work between public-data pipelines and consented Authenticated APIs on the eng team?”  
5. “What does a strong LLD answer look like on your team — more API/schema rigor, or more failure-mode depth?”  

## Avoid

- “What tech stack do you use?” as your only question  
- Compensation / HR questions in an LLD round  
- Anything you could answer from the website homepage in 10 seconds
